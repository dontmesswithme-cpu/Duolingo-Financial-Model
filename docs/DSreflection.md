## Active Learnings & Bug Prevention

- [P1.2] [Workflow] BUG: Forgot to arm watcher after submission — Director escalation "ARM WATCHER DUMBASS" -> Permanent Rule: After EVERY `SUBMISSION` or `RESUBMISSION` (and after every `PASS` wake that resets `status_op.json` to `idle`), immediately arm `node tools/watch_ds_inbox.mjs` with its native 2h watchdog (`WORKFLOW_WATCHER_TIMEOUT_MS=7200000`). Never use a short bash `timeout` that kills the watcher early (10s probe is not arming); never skip arming. Baseline is `status_ds.json.seq` at arm time; watcher wakes only on `seq > baseline`. Document baseline in `DSmemory.md` checklist every turn.
- [P1.2] [Transcription] BUG: `findInMap` used substring `includes` and fell through on dash → bottom-line total 661,311 mis-mapped to `convertible_preferred_stock` and `total_liabilities_and_stockholders_equity` left missing for FY2021 -> Permanent Rule: Anchor matches to row-start (`label === key || label.startsWith(key+' ')`), prefer longest key, normalize hyphens/apostrophes/commas in `normLabel`, preserve column positions (null for dash) and terminate on dash — never fall through to another row. Verbatim re-check must be column-pinned (`rawRow[column+1]` value AND printed form), not `table.text.includes`.
- [P1.4] [Sourcing/Testing] BUG: Growth fixture hand-typed fixture numbers instead of deriving from cited anchors at module load -> Permanent Rule: All derived fixtures must follow the `GROWTH_FIXTURE` / derived-anchor pattern (e.g. computed dynamically from `scheduleAnchor` rows). Never hand-type derived numbers in fixtures or tests.
- [P1.4] [Definitions] PATTERN: KPI / Statement definitions must be transcribed verbatim per the specific cited document and keyed per document. Never use a single global definition across filings with differing terminology.
- [P2.1] [Schedules/Validation] BUG: Missing 9M FY2025 income statement rows caused TTM revenue resolution to fail, and the engine silently fell back to single-quarter revenue (169% deferred revenue ratio) with 9 magic fallback literals in projectWorkingCapital -> Permanent Rule: Never fall back to quarterly denominators when a TTM basis is contracted; throw typed `EngineError` (`wc_ttm_denominator_unresolved`) fail-closed. In projection engines, require all drivers explicitly (`requireDriverValue`) throwing `EngineError` (`missing_driver`) rather than burying bare fallback literals in engine logic.
- [P2.1] [Data/Transcription] BUG: Storing the main-table aggregate "Other (1)" (123,565) under the metric key `revenue_other` collided with the corpus-wide meaning of `revenue_other` (the footnote 1 residual line, 1,410), corrupting derived Q4 and TTM `revenue_other` to negative values (−119,232) -> Permanent Rule: Never store aggregate parent subtotals under metric keys used across the corpus for granular residual lines. Transcribe the full companion decomposition (Advertising, DET, IAP, Other residual) and assert that all revenue components sum to total and remain strictly positive in every period.
- [P2.2] [Data/Transcription] PATTERN: PP&E and Intangible note breakdowns in 10-K/10-Q filings are presented on a gross basis with separate contra-asset lines (accumulated depreciation / amortization). Always transcribe both the gross components and contra lines, verify that components sum to gross, that gross + contra equals net, and that net matches the balance sheet face lines exactly across all periods.
- [P2.2] [Schedules/RollForward] PATTERN: Amortization expense disclosed in filing Notes (Note 5) reconciles with PP&E depreciation to match the Cash Flow Statement D&A line (Depreciation + Amortization = Total D&A). In roll-forward schedules, always compute additions from Cash Flow Statement investing outflows, depreciation / amortization from disclosures, and close the year-over-year roll-forward with explicitly computed disposals/adjustments (BOP + Additions - D&A + Disposals = EOP).
- [P2.2] [Schedules/CashFlowMapping] BUG: Schedule looked up `Q2 FY2026` in cash flow dataset instead of YTD span `6M FY2026`, resulting in 0 additions and hiding movements inside large plugs -> Permanent Rule: Balance sheet quarter dates (e.g. Q2 FY2026) must map to their corresponding YTD cash flow periods (`6M FY2026`) via explicit helper (`resolveCfPeriod`). Always assert additions equal filed cash flow rows and assert tight bounds on plugs.
- [P2.2] [Schedules/CorpusPurity] BUG: Hardcoded `FILED_AMORTIZATION` dictionary inside engine code bypassed data citation gates -> Permanent Rule: All filing note disclosures (e.g. amortization expense) must live in the cited dataset layer (`src/data/historical/income.json`) under schema validation and ledger enforcement. Engines must read them dynamically and enforce zero bare numeric literals > 999 via regression tests.
- [P2.2] [Schedules/RollForwardHonesty] BUG: Fabricating a beginning balance for FY2021 when FY2020 does not exist in corpus creates artificial roll-forward movement and plugs -> Permanent Rule: When prior period ending balance does not exist in corpus, mark `beginning_balance: { value: null }` and `isRollForwardClosed: false`. Start closed roll-forward from the first period with an established baseline (FY2022).
- [P2.3] [Schedules/DebtFreeProof] PATTERN: Proving a debt-free capital structure requires an active scan of all cited balance sheet dates and metrics rather than an empty table. Provide an enumerated scan showing zero funded debt, term loans, notes payable, or credit facility lines exist in any filing, accompanied by a regression tripwire asserting no future debt metric goes unnoticed.
- [P2.3] [Schedules/OperatingLeases] PATTERN: US GAAP ASC 842 operating leases (ROU assets and long-term operating lease liabilities) should be listed as filed in the debt schedule for visibility, accompanied by explicit disclosure that operating leases are not funded borrowings and that current lease obligations sit within accrued liabilities.
- [P2.3] [Schedules/SBC] PATTERN: Stock-based compensation expense flows from Cash Flow Statement operating activities, with discrete quarters and TTM derived via `ttm.compute` differencing. Dilution-context reference rows (stock option exercises, tax withholding share settlements, repurchases) provide complete capitalization context and should be carried from financing cash flows and labeled as reference.
- [P3.1] [Workflow/Watcher] RULE: Watchers (`node tools/watch_ds_inbox.mjs` / `watch_op_inbox.mjs`) must ALWAYS be executed foreground-blocking directly within the active tool call. Never run via background detached commands (`Start-Process`, `&`, background tasks). LLM agents are turn-based and only wake when a blocking tool call completes on signal receipt (`exit 0`).
- [P4.1] [FloatPrecision/Testing] PATTERN: Float tolerances must scale with magnitude. Multiplying large numbers like `148.36 × 50,031,000` lands on `...160.000001` as IEEE-754 double; flat `1e-12` absolute is unreachable at 7.4e9. Use `max(1e-12 absolute, 1e-12 relative)` so rates keep tight absolute bounds while large monetary values scale cleanly.
- [P4.1] [Validation/Gates] PATTERN: Two-stage fail-closed gates (e.g. `ConfigError` on missing driver in assumption discipline vs `EngineError` on non-finite value) require distinct test pins for both stages. Asserting only driver removal tests the discipline gate and leaves the value check vulnerable to regression.
- [P4.2] [DCF/NetCashBridge] PATTERN: General formula evaluation (`EV + netCash` with `netCash = cash + STI + LTI − debt`) must be evaluated on raw final forecast balance sheet components and verified with synthetic levered debt probes to prove the debt subtraction formula is active rather than hardcoding zero debt.
- [P4.3] [Scenarios/DriverPreservation] PATTERN: When `scenarios.apply` shifts driver values, it must preserve `marking`, `asOf`, and `source` metadata on the applied drivers so downstream modules like `wacc.build` retain MKT discipline checks under all scenarios.
