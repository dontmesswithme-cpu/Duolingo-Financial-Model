03Thesis

The franchise rests on a habit loop that converts casual learners into paying subscribers at a rate the market still discounts, and the operating model behind it scales with almost no marginal content cost. We therefore see the shares as undervalued against a base case built on modest user growth, steady conversion and a durable mix shift toward higher-priced tiers. The principal risk is not demand but the sustainability of engagement as the product matures, which we treat as a scenario input rather than a forecast.
