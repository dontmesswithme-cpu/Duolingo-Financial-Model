03 Investment thesis B##:H##

The subscription flywheel is the whole story — engagement compounds into paid conversion, and paid conversion compounds into pricing power, so nearly all of the value sits in the retention and conversion guesses rather than in the revenue base.

The base case assumes the company converts a stable share of its engagement into paid subscribers while holding gross margin, which is the single assumption that separates the bear case from the bull case.

The bear case is not a demand shock but a cost shock — content, model compute and marketing all scale with the user base, so a modest slip in margin or in the terminal growth guess removes most of the implied upside.
